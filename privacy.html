YOUR ENQUIRY.

Privacy contact details are loading. The form remains disabled until controller and retention information are configured.

What is processed and why

Name or alias, email address, optional team name, enquiry category and message are used to assess and respond to player, team and partnership enquiries. The intended basis for ordinary enquiries is legitimate interests in managing incoming communications; the operator must confirm this basis and any other applicable basis before publication.

Services and recipients

Vercel hosts this website and functions. Resend delivers the email to the club mailbox. Cloudflare Turnstile verifies submissions, and Upstash Redis holds short-lived request counters. The server derives a keyed hash of the connecting IP for rate limiting; counter records expire after up to 24 hours. Those hashes are not anonymous data. Message content is not intentionally stored in an application database. Hosting, challenge, mail and inbox providers may process technical logs and message data. The operator must confirm current provider contracts, processing locations and any required international-transfer safeguards before opening the form.

Your choices and rights

Do not include sensitive personal information in your introduction. Use the privacy contact above to request access, correction, deletion or restriction where applicable, or to object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may complain to the competent data-protection authority, including the Finnish Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman. Sending an enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing.

Website and analytics

This release includes no analytics scripts, externally hosted fonts or advertising trackers. The security widget loads only when the form is configured as open. Provider-side processing must be assessed separately. Social links lead to the providers only when you follow them.

The project

ONE GAME.
A LONGER VIEW.

INSTAGRAM ↗X / TWITTER ↗

YOKKO is being built from behind the scenes by someone with years around the Counter-Strike scene. The focus is CS2, not a collection of teams across different games.

The first goal is to become competitive online. From there, we want to develop the organisation piece by piece, at a pace the people and budget can support.

What we bring

Founder-funded practice-server and website costs, a shared identity, and practical organisation agreed with the players.

What we agree first

Practice commitment, goals, trial arrangements and responsibilities. Entry fees, travel and other costs are discussed separately.

What we don’t promise

Player salaries at this stage, guaranteed sponsors, or results. We start within our means.

Partnerships come next. If your business has a practical way to support this project, let’s talk.
